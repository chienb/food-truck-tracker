Welcome to Zero Gravitea!

Zero Gravitea is where flavor takes flight 🚀. We're a local food truck serving up out-of-this-world bites and drinks that lift your day a little higher. No fuss, no gravity—just good food, good vibes, and a whole lot of flavor. Our truck brings authentic Taiwanese street flavors to the neighborhood, with crispy popcorn chicken, juicy fried chicken cutlets, and more—made fresh and full of crunch. Step to the window, buckle in, and get ready for a taste adventure that's truly out of this world.
